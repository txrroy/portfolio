Leadership

The Team Sport Called Design

With only 8 designers in a 2,000+ person organization at VMware, I learned that the best outcomes happen when design is a collaborative effort. I built maturity programs that scaled impact beyond the design team - reinforcing my belief that design is a shared responsibility where everyone plays, solves, and grows together.

An Unique Perspective

The Accessible Advantage

I came to design not for how it looks, but for how well it works. As a colorblind designer (and developer), accessibility isn't just a checklist - it’s a lived constraint. This perspective drives me to build experiences that are usable, resilient, and inclusive by default.
